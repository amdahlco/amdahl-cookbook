Research the competitor: **$ARGUMENTS**

Use the Amdahl MCP. Do not use web_search alone; the value is the *divergence* between their public story and what prospects say about them on calls.

Run in parallel:

1. `external_search` — pull their current positioning, pricing posture, recent product launches, public reviews, and exec moves.
2. `data.search` — semantic search call transcripts and meeting notes for mentions of this competitor. Pull verbatim objections, comparisons, switch reasons, and pricing references.
3. `context.query_substrate` — ask the substrate for any persisted notes on win/loss against this competitor.

Output a 1-page brief: (a) their public narrative, (b) how prospects actually describe them (verbatim quotes with source IDs), (c) the gap, (d) 3 sharpened talk tracks for our reps to use in head-to-head deals.
