Build a positioning angle against: **$ARGUMENTS**

Do not invent positioning from generic frameworks. Pull the raw material from the Amdahl MCP first.

1. `data.search` — find call moments where prospects compared us to **$ARGUMENTS**. Extract verbatim language for both sides.
2. `data.query` — list closed-won and closed-lost deals where this competitor was in the mix. Note the stated reasons.
3. `context.query_substrate` — ask for the strongest differentiators that have actually moved deals (not aspirational ones).
4. `external_search` — confirm the competitor's current public positioning so we're countering the real thing, not a stale version.

Deliver: (a) their current public claim, (b) the 1-2 places they're genuinely strong, (c) the 2-3 places our corpus shows we win, (d) a tight positioning statement and 3 proof points (each tied to a specific call or deal ID), (e) a one-liner the rep can say on a discovery call.
