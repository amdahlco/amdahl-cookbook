Prepare for an upcoming meeting with: **$ARGUMENTS**

Use the Amdahl MCP for everything below; do not draft from general knowledge.

1. `context.summary` — load the account profile (stage, owner, ARR, last touch).
2. `data.query` — list all prior interactions (meetings, emails, support tickets) with this account, newest first. Note attendees on our side and theirs.
3. `data.search` — surface the last 3-5 meeting summaries and any unresolved questions or objections.
4. `external_search` — quick public-signal scan for anything new since our last touch (funding, exec changes, product news, hiring).

Produce: (a) account snapshot, (b) who's been involved on both sides, (c) open threads and last-stated objections, (d) what's new externally, (e) recommended 3-bullet agenda + 2 discovery questions tailored to where this deal actually is.
