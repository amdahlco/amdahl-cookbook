# Outbound targeting by signal

**What this does**: Builds a target list of companies that look like your best closed-won deals — based on the public + internal signal your own wins share.

**When to use it**: You're planning an outbound push and you want the list to be sharper than "fintech, 50-500 employees, anywhere in North America."

## Paste this into Claude

```
Look at our top closed-won deals in the last 12 months. What signal — public or otherwise — did they share before they bought? Funding stage, hiring patterns, tech stack moves, leadership changes, public commentary, anything. Build me a target profile from those shared signals. Then propose 25 companies that match the profile right now. For each one, tell me which signal they're showing and why they made the list.
```

## What you'll see back

- A profile of "what good looks like" — the shared signals across your wins.
- A list of 25 target companies that match.
- For each: which signals they show and a 1-line "why."
- A short note on signals that didn't matter (so you can stop targeting on them).

## Variations

- Smaller / sharper: `Give me 10 instead of 25, with deeper notes on each.`
- Segment lock: `Restrict to mid-market healthcare.`
- Add: `Suggest the right first-touch channel for each — email, LinkedIn, or warm intro.`

## Tips

- Best closed-won = your reference customers, not your biggest contracts.
- The "what didn't matter" callout is gold — most outbound is built on filters that don't actually correlate with wins.
- Pair with [personalize cold outreach](personalize-cold-outreach.md) for what to say to each one.
