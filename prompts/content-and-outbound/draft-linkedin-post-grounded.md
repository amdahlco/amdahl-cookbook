# Draft a LinkedIn post (grounded)

**What this does**: Writes a LinkedIn post in your brand voice, with at least one real customer phrase (anonymized) so it doesn't read like generic LinkedIn slop.

**When to use it**: You owe LinkedIn a post and you don't want it to sound like everyone else's LinkedIn post.

## Paste this into Claude

```
Draft a LinkedIn post about {topic}. Ground it in real customer language from our calls — pull at least one verbatim phrase (anonymized — no company or person names) that captures what buyers actually say about this topic. Match our brand voice. Keep it under 150 words. No emoji. No hashtags. End with a single question that invites a comment, not a CTA to "DM me."
```

## What you'll see back

- A post under 150 words.
- One (or more) anonymized buyer phrase woven in naturally.
- Voice that matches your brand (assuming the workspace has the voice configured).
- A genuine ending question, not a sales close.

## Variations

- Long form: `Write a 400-word version for our blog instead.`
- Contrarian: `Take a contrarian angle on the topic — what's the conventional wisdom and why is it wrong?`
- Founder voice: `Write it in {founder name}'s voice based on their past posts.`

## Tips

- "Anonymize the quote" is the unlock. Buyer language is what makes the post land; the company name is what makes it legal.
- If the first draft sounds generic, ask Claude to "make it more specific — what's the one detail nobody else would write?"
- Pair with [find content gaps](find-content-gaps.md) for what to actually post about.
