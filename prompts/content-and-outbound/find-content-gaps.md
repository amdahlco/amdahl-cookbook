# Find content gaps

**What this does**: Surfaces the questions, objections, and topics buyers keep raising on calls that your published content doesn't answer.

**When to use it**: You need a content roadmap and you'd like it grounded in something other than vibes.

## Paste this into Claude

```
Look at the questions, objections, and topics buyers raise repeatedly on our sales calls in the last 6 months. Cross-reference that with our published content — blog, docs, landing pages. Tell me: which buyer questions do we cover well? Which do we cover poorly? Which do we not cover at all? Rank the gaps by how often they come up. End with 5 specific content ideas with working titles and one sentence on the angle.
```

## What you'll see back

- Three buckets: covered well / covered poorly / not covered.
- Ranked gap list with frequency counts.
- 5 specific content ideas with titles and angles.
- Often: one or two "you already wrote about this but nobody can find it" SEO gaps.

## Variations

- Channel-specific: `Only the topics that would work as LinkedIn posts.`
- Sales enablement: `What's missing as a one-pager / battle card?`
- Add: `Tell me which of these would also help our SDRs in cold outreach.`

## Tips

- Frequency matters more than recency. A question asked 30 times last year beats one asked once last week.
- If two gaps overlap, ask Claude to "consolidate into a single piece" before you commission three blog posts.
- Pair with [draft a LinkedIn post](draft-linkedin-post-grounded.md) to actually ship one.
