# What they care about

**What this does**: Surfaces the 3 themes one account keeps coming back to — across calls, emails, and CRM notes — so you can stop guessing their priorities.

**When to use it**: You've talked to this account a few times but the threads feel scattered. You want to know what actually matters to *them*, not what you've been pitching.

## Paste this into Claude

```
For {company name or domain}, look across every call, email, and CRM note we have. What are the 3 themes or priorities they keep coming back to — in their own words? For each theme, give me 2-3 verbatim quotes (with who said it, and roughly when), and one sentence on how serious they sound about it.
```

## What you'll see back

- Three themes, ranked by how often / recently they come up.
- Verbatim quotes attached to each theme, with speaker and rough date.
- A "seriousness" read: is this a passing comment or a board-level priority?
- Often: a fourth "honorable mention" theme worth tracking.

## Variations

- Narrow to one role: `Only count things said by their VP of Engineering or above.`
- Narrow the window: `Only consider the last 6 months.`
- Compare: `Then tell me which of these themes our messaging actually addresses.`

## Tips

- If a theme feels vague ("scalability"), ask Claude to "tell me what they specifically mean when they say this" — quotes get sharper.
- Cross-check with the executive sponsor's LinkedIn — themes often match their stated priorities.
