# Whose name comes up

**What this does**: Surfaces the people, vendors, or topics that get mentioned around an account — even when nobody flagged them.

**When to use it**: You suspect there's a quiet decision-maker, a competitor in the wings, or a tool in their stack that nobody's named in the CRM. You want the hidden context.

## Paste this into Claude

```
For {company name or domain}, scan every call and note we have. Tell me which people, vendors, tools, or competitors are mentioned — even briefly — that aren't already on the deal's contact list or marked as a competitor in the CRM. For each one, tell me how often they came up, who brought them up, and what was said. Rank by how often they appear.
```

## What you'll see back

- A ranked list of names — people, vendors, tools, competitors.
- Frequency, speaker, and the actual context they came up in.
- Quotes where the mention was meaningful.
- A short "worth chasing" callout for the top 1-2.

## Variations

- People only: `Just the human names — internal champions or skeptics we haven't logged as contacts.`
- Vendors only: `Just the other tools in their stack we've heard about.`
- Add: `Tell me which of these we should ask about on the next call.`

## Tips

- This is where shadow champions and shadow blockers come out. Take the top 1-2 seriously.
- If someone's name keeps coming up and they're not on the CRM, that's the first follow-up.
- Pair with [deep-dive on account](deep-dive-on-account.md) for the full picture.
