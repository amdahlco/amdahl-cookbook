# Prospect cold research

**What this does**: First read on a company you have no history with — so you walk into the first call already smarter than the rep before you.

**When to use it**: A new logo just hit your queue or your inbox. You have 10 minutes before the call.

## Paste this into Claude

```
We have no internal history with {company name or domain}. Build me a cold-research brief: what they do, who their customers are, how they make money, recent funding and hiring, their leadership team, the 3 things they're publicly prioritizing right now, and any signal that they might need what we sell. Also check if any of our existing customers, partners, or accounts mention them — even in passing — on calls or in CRM notes.
```

## What you'll see back

- A short "what they do, who they sell to, how they make money" paragraph.
- Recent public signal: funding, hiring, leadership, product.
- A "why they might care about us" section tied to your offering.
- A bonus section: any time this company has shown up adjacent to your existing customers.

## Variations

- For investor-style research: `End with a SWOT.`
- For SDR-style: `End with 3 cold-email opening lines tailored to the strongest signal.`
- For partnerships: `Focus on integrations, channel partners, and tech stack.`

## Tips

- A domain is more reliable than a name (lots of companies share names).
- Add the country or HQ city if the name is generic.
- If they're stealth or pre-launch, ask: "what can we infer from the team's backgrounds?"
