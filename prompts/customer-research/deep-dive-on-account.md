# Deep-dive on an account

**What this does**: Builds a full picture of one company — your history with them and everything happening publicly right now — in a single brief.

**When to use it**: You're about to spend real time on this account (a big pitch, a board mention, a save play) and a 5-minute Google isn't enough.

## Paste this into Claude

```
Pull everything we know about {company name or domain}. Fuse our internal history — past deals, support tickets, who we've talked to, what they said on calls, what's open and what's stalled — with what's happening publicly right now (funding, hiring, product launches, leadership changes, recent news). Give me a 1-page brief: where they are in their buying journey with us, the 3 things they care about most, the 2 risks I should know about, and a recommended next step.
```

## What you'll see back

- A 1-page brief, not an essay.
- Internal section: deal stage, last meaningful touch, named contacts, open objections, sentiment.
- External section: recent funding, hiring signal, product moves, leadership change.
- A short "what to do next" recommendation tied to both.

## Variations

- Add `Focus the public signal on the last 90 days only.` if the account is fast-moving.
- Swap the close: `End with 5 talking points I can use in a meeting tomorrow.` for sales prep.
- Add `Flag anything that contradicts what they've told us on calls.` to surface divergence.

## Tips

- Give a full company name or a domain — not just "they" or an abbreviation. Disambiguation is where this breaks.
- If the brief comes back thin, follow up with "pull more verbatim quotes from our calls" — that almost always unlocks more.
- If the account is huge (multi-division), name the division or business unit too.
