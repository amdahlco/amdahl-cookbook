# Deals at risk

**What this does**: Surfaces deals that look healthy on paper — right stage, recent activity, no flag in the CRM — but are actually at risk based on what's being said (or not said) on calls.

**When to use it**: Forecast week. Or any time the pipeline looks too clean to be true.

## Paste this into Claude

```
Show me the deals in our pipeline that look healthy on paper but are at risk based on call activity, sentiment, unanswered objections, champion changes, or competitive mentions. For each one: deal name, stage, ACV if known, last meaningful touch, the specific signal that worries you, and what the rep should do this week to save it. Sort by ACV times risk.
```

## What you'll see back

- A short list — usually 3-8 deals — each with a specific risk signal.
- The "looks healthy because" + "but actually" framing for each.
- A specific suggested action per deal.
- Sorted so the biggest at-risk dollars are at the top.

## Variations

- Cohort: `Only deals expected to close this quarter.`
- Stage: `Only deals in late stage (post-demo or further).`
- Add: `For each one, draft the email or Slack message the rep should send today.`

## Tips

- Champion-gone-quiet is the single highest-precision risk signal. Take it seriously.
- "No bad signals" is itself a signal in late-stage deals — silence isn't peace.
- If the same risk shows up across 3+ deals, that's a [pattern across cohort](../win-loss-deal-postmortem/pattern-across-cohort.md) question.
