# Recipe library

24 copy-paste recipes for go-to-market work. Each recipe is a self-contained markdown file with one paste-ready prompt, a description of what comes back, a few variations, and tips.

New here? Start with the [main README](../README.md) for setup and the six headliner prompts.

---

## Customer research (4)

Know an account — yours or a prospect's — before you walk in.

- [Deep-dive on account](customer-research/deep-dive-on-account.md) — full picture of an existing or prospect account, fused with public signal.
- [Prospect cold research](customer-research/prospect-cold-research.md) — first read on a company you have no history with.
- [What they care about](customer-research/what-they-care-about.md) — the 3 themes this account keeps coming back to.
- [Whose name comes up](customer-research/whose-name-comes-up.md) — find the people, vendors, or topics that quietly shape an account.

## Competitive intel (3)

Their public posture vs. how buyers actually describe them.

- [Competitor deep-dive](competitive-intel/competitor-deep-dive.md) — public story + how they're discussed inside your deals.
- [Where they show up in our calls](competitive-intel/where-they-show-up-in-our-calls.md) — every call this competitor was mentioned on, and what was said.
- [How buyers compare us](competitive-intel/how-buyers-compare-us.md) — the verbatim trade-offs buyers make between you and them.

## Positioning & messaging (4)

Pressure-test copy against the language your customers actually use.

- [Audit our positioning](positioning-messaging/audit-our-positioning.md) — homepage / one-pager vs. real customer voice.
- [Rep vs. buyer language](positioning-messaging/rep-vs-buyer-language.md) — where your reps talk past how buyers describe the problem.
- [Stress-test a message](positioning-messaging/stress-test-a-message.md) — paste a draft, get back the parts that won't land and why.
- [Rebuild your value narrative by segment](positioning-messaging/rebuild-value-narrative-by-segment.md) — pull raw customer voice per ICP segment with anti-contamination filters; the raw material for a positioning pivot.

## Pre-meeting prep (3)

Walk in knowing the room.

- [Sales call prep](pre-meeting-prep/sales-call-prep.md) — 1-page brief for tomorrow's discovery or pitch.
- [QBR prep](pre-meeting-prep/qbr-prep.md) — recap, risks, expansion angles, and a recommended agenda.
- [Renewal prep](pre-meeting-prep/renewal-prep.md) — the renewal conversation, grounded in the actual year.

## Win/loss & deal postmortem (3)

Real reasons, not dropdown reasons.

- [Why we lost this deal](win-loss-deal-postmortem/why-we-lost-this-deal.md) — the honest story of one closed-lost deal.
- [Pattern across cohort](win-loss-deal-postmortem/pattern-across-cohort.md) — common threads across a set of wins or losses.
- [What separates won from lost](win-loss-deal-postmortem/what-separates-won-from-lost.md) — the differences between deals that closed and ones that didn't.

## Content & outbound (4)

Grounded posts, smarter targeting, less generic outreach.

- [Draft a LinkedIn post (grounded)](content-and-outbound/draft-linkedin-post-grounded.md) — your voice, anchored in real customer language.
- [Find content gaps](content-and-outbound/find-content-gaps.md) — questions buyers keep asking that your content doesn't answer.
- [Outbound targeting by signal](content-and-outbound/outbound-targeting-by-signal.md) — a target list that looks like your best closed-won deals.
- [Personalize cold outreach](content-and-outbound/personalize-cold-outreach.md) — a non-generic opener per prospect.

## Pipeline pulse (3)

Weekly health, risk, triage.

- [Weekly recap](pipeline-pulse/weekly-recap.md) — what moved, what stalled, what to watch.
- [Deals at risk](pipeline-pulse/deals-at-risk.md) — pipeline that looks healthy but isn't.
- [Stalled pipeline triage](pipeline-pulse/stalled-pipeline-triage.md) — what to do about the deals that have gone quiet.
