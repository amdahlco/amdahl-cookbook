# Where they show up in our calls

**What this does**: Lists every call this competitor was mentioned on — who brought them up, in what context, and what was said.

**When to use it**: You want to know if a competitor is gaining ground, losing ground, or showing up in deal stages they didn't used to.

## Paste this into Claude

```
List every call in the last 6 months where {competitor name} was mentioned. For each one: deal name, date, deal stage at the time, who on the customer side raised them, and 1-2 verbatim quotes of what was said. End with a summary: are they coming up earlier, later, more, less? Which deal stages? Which segments?
```

## What you'll see back

- A chronological table of mentions: deal, date, stage, speaker, quote.
- A trend summary: more/less, earlier/later, which segments.
- Notable single quotes pulled out.
- 1-2 hypotheses about *why* the pattern looks the way it does.

## Variations

- Cohort: `Only deals over $100K ACV.`
- Outcome: `Only on calls that resulted in closed-lost.`
- Cross-cut: `Group by deal stage instead of by date.`

## Tips

- If the competitor's name is a common word (a person's name, a verb), specify the domain.
- Run this monthly for top competitors to spot drift.
- A sudden jump in stage-2 mentions usually means they've launched something — chase it.
