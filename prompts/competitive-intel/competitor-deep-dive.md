# Competitor deep-dive

**What this does**: Two-column picture of a competitor — their public story on one side, how prospects and customers actually describe them on your calls on the other.

**When to use it**: You're updating a battle card, prepping for a deal where this competitor is in the mix, or building a new positioning narrative.

## Paste this into Claude

```
Build me a deep-dive on {competitor name or domain}. Give me two sides: (1) their public posture — positioning, pricing tier, target ICP, recent product moves, marketing themes — and (2) how prospects and customers actually describe them on our calls — what they say is good, what they say is broken, why they win, why they lose. End with the 3 biggest divergences between the public story and the call reality.
```

## What you'll see back

- A "public story" column: positioning, ICP, pricing posture, recent moves.
- A "call reality" column: verbatim quotes from your calls.
- A divergence section: where the two stories don't match.
- 2-3 angles to attack or sidestep, depending on where the divergence sits.

## Variations

- Narrow scope: `Focus only on deals we lost to them in the last 12 months.`
- Industry slice: `Only on calls with mid-market healthcare prospects.`
- Add: `End with a 3-line battle card I can paste into Slack.`

## Tips

- If the competitor has multiple product lines, name the one you care about — otherwise the divergences get muddled.
- Quotes are gold. Ask for more if the first pass feels generic.
- Pair with [where they show up in our calls](where-they-show-up-in-our-calls.md) for the volume view.
