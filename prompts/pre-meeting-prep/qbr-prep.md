# QBR prep

**What this does**: Builds the recap, the risk list, the expansion angles, and a recommended agenda for a customer QBR.

**When to use it**: A few days before a QBR — you want to walk in knowing the wins to celebrate, the risks to flag, and the next thing to sell.

## Paste this into Claude

```
I have a QBR with {company name} on {date}. Build me the prep: (1) a recap of the last quarter — what they used, what worked, what didn't, what they said on calls; (2) risks I need to flag — sentiment changes, unanswered objections, contract concerns; (3) expansion angles — based on what they've said they care about, what's the next thing worth pitching; (4) a recommended agenda. End with the 3 quotes from the quarter I should be ready to reference.
```

## What you'll see back

- Quarter recap: usage signal, milestones, escalations.
- Risk section: sentiment shifts, unresolved issues, contract red flags.
- Expansion: 1-2 angles grounded in what they've actually said.
- Recommended agenda with rough timing.

## Variations

- Multi-stakeholder: `Tell me what each attendee specifically cares about based on past calls.`
- Renewal coming up: `Flag anything that affects the renewal conversation.`
- Add: `Draft an opening recap slide in 5 bullets.`

## Tips

- If the customer has multiple business units, name the one this QBR covers.
- Expansion angles work best when they're grounded in a quote — ask for one if it's missing.
- For the renewal version, use [renewal prep](renewal-prep.md) instead.
