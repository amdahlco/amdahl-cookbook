# Sales call prep

**What this does**: 1-page brief for tomorrow's call — who's in the room, what's been said, what's open, what to say next.

**When to use it**: The night before (or 20 minutes before) any meaningful sales call — discovery, demo, follow-up.

## Paste this into Claude

```
I have a {discovery / demo / follow-up} call with {company name or domain} on {date or "tomorrow"}. Build me a 1-page prep brief: who's attending and what each person cares about, our full history with them, the last 3 things they said that matter, any open objections, what stage the deal is in, and a recommended agenda for the call. Include 3-5 verbatim quotes from previous calls I should keep in mind.
```

## What you'll see back

- Attendees and what each one cares about (where known).
- A short history: stage, last touch, deal value if it exists.
- The last 3 meaningful things they said, in their own words.
- Open objections + a recommended agenda.

## Variations

- New attendee: `Include a mini-bio on {new person's name} — their role, what they're likely to care about, any public signal.`
- Demo: `Recommend which 3 demo flows to prioritize for this specific call.`
- Add: `End with 5 discovery questions tailored to what's still unknown.`

## Tips

- Name the meeting type — discovery vs. demo vs. negotiation needs different prep.
- If the call is in 20 minutes, ask Claude to "keep it to 200 words." You'll actually read it.
- After the call, follow up with [QBR prep](qbr-prep.md) or [renewal prep](renewal-prep.md) for downstream stages.
