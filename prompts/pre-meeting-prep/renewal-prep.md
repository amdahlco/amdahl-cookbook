# Renewal prep

**What this does**: A prep doc for the renewal conversation, grounded in the actual year — what they used, what they complained about, what they got value from, what to push for.

**When to use it**: 30-60 days before a renewal, especially if it's a big one or if the sentiment has been mixed.

## Paste this into Claude

```
The renewal with {company name} is coming up on {renewal date}. Build me a renewal prep doc: what they actually used this year, the value moments and the friction moments (with quotes), the sentiment trajectory across the year, anyone we've lost or gained as a champion, any competitive mentions, and a recommended renewal posture — flat, expand, or defend. End with the 3 strongest things I can point to as proof of value.
```

## What you'll see back

- Usage and adoption signal across the year.
- Value moments with quotes; friction moments with quotes.
- Sentiment trajectory — getting warmer, colder, flat.
- Champion changes (gained, lost, gone quiet).
- A posture recommendation: flat / expand / defend.

## Variations

- Multi-year: `Compare year 1 to year 2 — what changed?`
- Multi-product: `Break it down by product line they have with us.`
- Add: `Draft the renewal email I'd send their economic buyer next week.`

## Tips

- Champion changes are the single biggest renewal signal. Don't skip that section.
- If the sentiment trajectory is flat, ask Claude to "tell me what would have to be true for them to upgrade." That sets the year-2 plan.
- For mid-year health, run [QBR prep](qbr-prep.md) instead.
